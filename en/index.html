Questions

Do I need to know mechanics already?

No. The first mission starts with a wheel and a shaft, and every idea comes with its explanation, diagrams and a glossary.

Which devices?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, in portrait and landscape.

Do I need an account or a connection?

No. Rouages works offline, with no account. Your progress and builds stay on your device.

Is my data collected?

No. The app collects no data. The details are in the privacy policy.
